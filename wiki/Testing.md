# Testing

1. Register a venue.
2. Create an event with a clear title.
3. Confirm tab completion shows the title rather than an ID.
4. Buy two tickets with the same player and confirm two distinct paper tickets are issued.
5. Confirm each paper shows the event name and contains its hidden identifiers.
6. Admit one ticket and verify the second remains valid.
7. Try to reuse the admitted ticket and confirm it fails.
8. Have the host reserve giveaway tickets.
9. Sell event tickets through a sign shop.
10. At the scheduled start time, confirm online ticket holders hear the chime.
11. Cancel a future event and confirm valid paid tickets are refunded exactly once.
